# Behavioral Observation - Night Shift Restart Work

Document ID: behavioral-observation-night-restart
Domain: human_factors
Observation window: 2026-05-10 to 2026-05-21
Area: Machining cell, line 2

## Observation purpose

This behavioral observation summarizes system conditions observed during night-shift restart work. It is not an individual performance record.

## Observed work-design patterns

- Night shift uses shared inspection and setup support across adjacent lines.
- Restart checks are usually completed correctly when the handover note explicitly says the line remains in restart status.
- When the handover note says only "returned to production," the receiving shift may treat the line as stable unless the remaining verification step is separately called out.
- Operators reported that the part-10 check is easiest to miss when service work finishes in the final hour of the prior shift.

## Positive controls observed

- Teams escalated clearly when a formal quality hold was present.
- SPC reaction-plan steps were understood across all shifts.
- No observed pattern suggests lack of basic line-operation skill.

## RCA relevance

The relevant human-factors signal is the interaction between lean-night support coverage and handover specificity. The observation supports system-level findings about work design, not person-level causation.
