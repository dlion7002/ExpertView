# Procedure - CNC Post-Maintenance Verification

Document ID: procedure-post-maintenance-verification
Domain: human_factors
Procedure owner: Manufacturing operations
Revision: PMV-CNC-L2-2026.04

## Scope

This procedure applies whenever a CNC machine returns to production after maintenance that can affect axis motion, hydraulic clamping, spindle loading, or gauging reliability.

## Required verification cadence

1. Maintenance release confirms the asset is safe to run.
2. Operations confirms the restart setup and material identity.
3. The first piece after service is measured before release.
4. Parts 5 and 10 are measured and recorded during the first hour after restart.
5. Normal SPC cadence resumes only after the part-10 restart verification is recorded within specification.

## Role coverage

- The primary line operation role runs the restart sequence.
- The inspection role records first-piece and part-10 measurements.
- The setup or quality support role confirms any post-maintenance adjustment.
- The shift lead confirms that the restart verification is complete before stable-production status is recorded.

## Shift-boundary handover rule

If post-maintenance verification crosses a shift boundary, the outgoing shift must state the current verification step and the remaining required checks. The receiving shift must treat the line as in restart status until the part-10 verification is documented.

## System-level control intent

The procedure is designed to catch drift created by service work before the line returns to routine cadence. The control depends on staffing coverage, certification coverage, and explicit handover language when service work occurs near shift change.
