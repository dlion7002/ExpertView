# Learning Team Report - Post-Maintenance Verification Near Miss

Document ID: learning-team-post-maintenance-near-miss
Domain: human_factors
Report date: 2026-04-18
Area: Machining cell

## Event summary

A prior near miss occurred when a machine returned from maintenance near a shift boundary and the receiving shift resumed routine production cadence before the part-10 verification was documented. The issue was caught before shipment by a downstream containment check.

## Learning-team themes

- Service events near shift change require stronger handover language than routine production updates.
- Lean-night staffing reduces redundancy in post-maintenance verification checks.
- The phrase "returned to production" is ambiguous unless paired with the current verification step.
- Teams asked for handover notes to separate "maintenance release" from "stable production release."

## Corrective actions

- Add explicit restart-status language to shift handover notes.
- Reinforce that part-10 verification is required before routine SPC cadence resumes.
- Review certification overlap for night-shift restart work.

## RCA relevance

This report establishes that compressed handover language around post-maintenance verification was a known system vulnerability before the CNC line 2 incident. It does not identify or evaluate individuals.
