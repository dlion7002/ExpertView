## Shift 3 coverage note

Shift 3 has complete standard operation coverage and complete SPC reaction-plan coverage. The thinner point is overlap between first-piece-after-service inspection and post-maintenance verification during lean-night staffing. The standard work expectation is that the handover note explicitly flags any restart that requires the first-hour verification cadence.

## RCA relevance

- The certification matrix does not indicate a lack of basic CNC operation skill.
- It does indicate less redundant coverage for post-maintenance verification on shift 3.
- The matrix depends on clear handover communication when service work crosses a shift boundary.
