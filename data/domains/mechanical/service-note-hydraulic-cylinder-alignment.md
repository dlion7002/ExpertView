# Service Note: Hydraulic Cylinder Alignment After Seal Replacement

Document ID: `mech-service-note-hydraulic-cylinder-alignment`
Vendor style: Internal maintenance standard
Applies to: Line 2 fixture hydraulic cylinders
Revision: 2026-04-28

## Purpose

Hydraulic cylinder seal replacement can disturb rod-end alignment even when the cylinder body is returned to the original mounting holes. A small angular bias at the rod-end clevis may shift fixture clamp position enough to affect finished-part dimensions.

## Required checks

1. Verify rod extension at low pressure before reconnecting the clevis.
2. Align clevis witness marks to the prior paint reference.
3. Torque rod-end locknut to 42 N-m unless the asset-specific card states otherwise.
4. Bleed the hydraulic circuit until clamp pressure trace settles without visible ripple.
5. Run ten dry cycles and record the final two clamp-settle traces.
6. Complete a ten-part capability check before releasing production when the service occurs during shift 3.

## Warning signs

- Short oscillation at clamp settle after a seal-kit service.
- First parts measure in tolerance, then drift as fixture temperature and pressure stabilize.
- Operator reports "clamp chatter" immediately before cut starts.

## RCA guidance

Hydraulic alignment should be the first check when dimensional drift starts immediately after cylinder maintenance. If unloaded fixture return is stable and chatter grows after spindle warm-up, continue the RCA toward spindle bearing preload, bearing fit, or bearing batch history.
