# Qualification Addendum: Bearing Supplier - North PPAP Review

Document ID: `qualification-addendum-bearing-supplier-north-ppap`
Area: Supplier quality qualification
Review date: 2026-05-16
Supplier alias: `Bearing Supplier - North`
Related item family: Line 2 CNC spindle support bearings

## Purpose

This addendum records the supplier-quality review completed after `Bearing Supplier - North` submitted its production part approval packet for the line 2 spindle support bearing family. It supplements the controlled-use qualification packet and does not grant unrestricted substitution.

## Submitted evidence

| Packet item | Supplier submission | Plant review note | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dimensional capability summary | 30-piece capability run | Cpk reported above 1.33 on print dimensions | Accepted with monitoring |
| Material certificate | Heat lot certificate attached | Certificate matched purchase order family | Accepted |
| Process flow | Turning, heat treat, grind, wash, grease, pack | Grease application step relies on operator visual confirmation | Accepted with audit note |
| Control plan | Critical dimensions and rotation torque | Rotation torque plan uses supplier-local baseline | Accepted with comparison request |
| Packaging trial | Sleeve pack with corrosion wrap | No corrosion indicator concern in trial | Accepted |

## Open qualification controls

- First three production lots remain under elevated receiving QA sampling.
- Plant receiving QA must compare rotation feel and grease film against retained regular-source samples.
- Maintenance planners must preserve lot traceability by asset during the two-week monitoring window.
- Quality engineering must reopen this addendum if early chatter, warm-runout growth, or preload adjustment difficulty appears on an asset using a first-use lot.

## Review note

The PPAP packet supports controlled use of `Bearing Supplier - North`, but the grease application and rotation-torque comparison points remain monitoring items. These notes are intended to guide evidence review, not to conclude supplier fault.
