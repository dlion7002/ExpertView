# Supplier Audit Log: Bearing Supplier - North 2026 Q1

Document ID: `supplier-audit-log-bearing-supplier-north-2026-q1`
Area: Supplier quality audit
Audit date: 2026-03-28
Supplier alias: `Bearing Supplier - North`
Auditor: Supplier quality engineering

## Audit scope

The Q1 audit covered the bearing wash, grease application, final rotation check, packaging, and lot-traceability controls used for spindle support bearing families. The audit was performed before the May controlled-use release for line 2.

## Observations

| Area | Observation | Severity | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grease application | Operator visual standard was present, but the retained master photo was older than the current grease supplier lot. | Minor | Update requested before unrestricted approval. |
| Rotation check | Final rotation torque checks were recorded, but the control chart used supplier-local history rather than plant retained samples. | Minor | Plant comparison required for first three lots. |
| Lot traceability | Heat lot and packaging lot were traceable through the traveler. | None | Accepted. |
| Nonconformance handling | Rework bins were labeled and segregated. | None | Accepted. |
| Packaging | Corrosion wrap and sleeve protection were in place. | None | Accepted. |

## Follow-up actions

- Supplier quality requested an updated grease-film visual standard before removing controlled-use status.
- Receiving QA added a plant-side comparison check for first-use lots.
- Purchasing retained the supplier on backup status until three monitored lots complete without a line-impacting hold.

## Audit conclusion

No major finding blocked controlled use. The open minor observations are most relevant when an RCA involves early chatter, warm-runout growth, grease-film comparison, or rotation-feel differences on a first-use bearing lot.
