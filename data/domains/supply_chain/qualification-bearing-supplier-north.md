# Supplier Qualification: Bearing Supplier - North

Document ID: `qualification-bearing-supplier-north`
Area: Supplier quality
Effective date: 2026-05-12
Supplier alias: `Bearing Supplier - North`

## Scope

`Bearing Supplier - North` is qualified as a backup supplier for spindle support bearings used on line 2 CNC assets. Qualification is limited to controlled batches until three consecutive lots pass receiving QA without a line-impacting quality hold.

## Qualification basis

| Requirement | Target | Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Certificate of conformance | Required per lot | Supplier certificate template reviewed | Accepted |
| Process capability | Cpk >= 1.33 on critical dimensions | Supplier summary submitted | Accepted with monitoring |
| Sample-size plan | First three lots require elevated receiving sample | Plant QA plan attached | Accepted |
| Lot traceability | Asset-level trace for first two weeks | Purchasing and crib process updated | Accepted |
| First-article review | Required before unrestricted use | See first-article packet | Conditional |

## Open controls

- First three released lots require receiving QA sample checks at the elevated sample level.
- Maintenance must record the batch ID on the spindle service traveler.
- Quality engineering should review any early chatter, warm-runout growth, or repeated adjustment difficulty before moving the supplier to unrestricted status.

## Qualification note

The supplier is qualified for backup use, not unrestricted substitution. The qualification packet does not waive asset-level traceability during the monitoring window.
