# Corrective Action Record: Prior Supplier Transition Controls

Document ID: `corrective-action-prior-supplier-transition`
Area: Supplier quality corrective action
Closed date: 2026-03-06
Reference: `SC-CAPA-2026-014`

## Background

A prior temporary supplier transition on line 1 created avoidable troubleshooting delay because receiving QA released a qualified alternate source but maintenance records did not preserve lot traceability by asset. The issue was closed after purchasing, QA, and maintenance planning updated the transition-control checklist.

## Containment actions

| Action | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Require batch ID on maintenance traveler for first-use lots | Maintenance planning | Complete |
| Keep first-use lots separate in crib inventory | Stores | Complete |
| Attach receiving QA packet to quality holds during monitoring window | Receiving QA | Complete |
| Review supplier transition in RCA if symptoms appear within two weeks | Quality engineering | Complete |

## Lesson learned

Supplier transitions can add investigation noise even when the incoming lot passes print requirements. The corrective action does not treat alternate suppliers as defective; it requires cleaner traceability so quality events can distinguish part variation, installation factors, and process drift.

## Applicability

The checklist applies to any temporary supplier transition for CNC spindle, clamp, or hydraulic components used on lines 1 and 2.
