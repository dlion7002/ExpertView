# Prior Bearing Batch Incident Summary: Line 1 Warm-Runout Review

Document ID: `prior-bearing-batch-incident-2025`
Area: Supplier quality history
Incident window: 2025-11-03 through 2025-11-08
Asset family: CNC spindle support bearings

## Background

Line 1 opened a quality hold in November 2025 after two CNC assets showed warm-runout growth and light chatter within one week of a bearing replacement campaign. The affected bearings came from the regular bearing source, not `Bearing Supplier - North`.

## Findings from the prior review

| Evidence area | Summary |
|---|---|
| Receiving QA | The lot passed print checks and certificate review. |
| Maintenance | Both affected assets used the same bearing lot and the same preload procedure. |
| Mechanical reliability | Warm-runout growth appeared only after spindle warm-up, not during unloaded fixture checks. |
| Supplier quality | Retained samples from the lot had rotation feel near the low end of the regular-source historical range. |
| Corrective action | Receiving QA added retained-sample comparison notes for rotation feel and grease film on monitored lots. |

## Resolution

The 2025 review did not assign the issue to supplier quality alone. The final report treated lot-level variation and installation preload sensitivity as interacting contributors. The corrective action focused on traceability and comparison checks so future RCAs could separate supplier-history clues from maintenance setup clues.

## Applicability note

Use this summary as historical context when a current incident combines bearing replacement, early chatter, warm-runout growth, and a monitored or first-use lot. It is not evidence that any current supplier caused the incident.
