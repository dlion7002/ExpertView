## Scope

This excerpt lists process controls that apply to line 2 CNC-04 when machining hydraulic-valve sleeve bores. It does not replace mechanical troubleshooting; it defines how the process is kept inside control while production continues.

## Special characteristics

| Characteristic | Process control | Verification frequency | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Finished bore diameter | In-process bore gauge and offset trend chart | Restart sequence plus hourly after release | Process technician |
| Fixture clamp settle | Clamp pressure trace reviewed after service | First-good-part and part 10 after maintenance | Shift lead |
| Roughing-pass chatter note | Operator entry in inspection log | Every measured part during restart sequence | Operator |
| Surface finish banding | Visual check with tagged sample tray | First-good-part and every tenth part | Quality technician |

## Release rule after maintenance

Production may not exceed ten parts after maintenance until the restart sequence has three conforming bore measurements and no unreviewed chatter note. If the part-10 entry is missing, the station stays in controlled restart status and the next completed part must be measured before additional release.

## Escalation path

The process engineer owns line clearance when dimensional drift is paired with either fixture clamp settle notes or roughing-pass chatter notes. The shift lead may contain product, but only process engineering can close the reaction record.
